# Sober Girl keyword gaps vs sobergirlsociety.com (verified 2026-10-10)

**Data:** DataForSEO, US and UK, pulled 2026-10-10 with `scripts/dataforseo-keyword-gaps.mjs`. Spend about $0.30. Raw files are in `research/`:
`sobergirlsociety-ranked-2026-10-10.csv` (256 keywords they rank for, positions 1 to 30), `sobergirl-candidates-2026-10-10.csv` (62 terms), `sobergirl-terms-round2-2026-10-10.csv` (84 terms).

How to read the numbers: volumes are US monthly searches from Google Ads data (competitor rows use the market named in the row). KD is DataForSEO's difficulty, which is not comparable to Ahrefs or Semrush. A blank volume means Google reports nothing measurable, which is why the many blanks below are real "no demand" answers, not missing data.

## What the data says about them

- **They are a community, events and shop brand.** No app. Their blog is mostly lifestyle writing.
- **Half of what they rank for is non-alcoholic drinks.** 127 of 256 ranked keywords are vodka, gin, prosecco, wine and similar.
- **They rank for "N years sober" with merch pages.** Their pin and keyring product pages sit at positions 3 to 5 for "7 years sober", "9 years sober" and "1 year sober" (UK). A weak page type, ranking because nobody else wrote the page.
- **They rank for no tracker or app keyword.** "Sobriety tracker", "sober counter", "quit drinking app": none. We are not up against them there.
- **We rank for none of their keywords yet** (`we_rank` is "no" on all 256). Pages are unindexed or too new; check again in 4 to 6 weeks.

Two cautions: the app has no badge past 365 days (adding 2, 5 and 10 year badges would make these pages truthful about the product), and a person at five years is more often looking for a gift or a message than a tracker, so keep each page short and link to the anniversary page.

By far the largest informational pool in the data, and nobody at this KD is a tracker app. It fits our audience (women noticing what drinking does to their face and gut) and it feeds the "benefits of quitting" story. It was skipped before because we had no citable source. The volume now justifies finding one: dermatology or gastroenterology review articles, not blog posts. Every claim must carry a citation, as on the other guides.

### 3. Relapse and slips (new page, strong tracker fit)

| Query | Searches | KD |
|---|---|---|
| relapse | 33,100 | 49 |
| alcohol relapse | 720 | 21 |
| lapse vs relapse | 720 | 4 |
| i relapsed | 480 | 28 |

"Relapse" alone is too hard and too broad. A page built on "lapse vs relapse" (KD 4) and "alcohol relapse", with "I relapsed: what to do next" as the main section, targets about 1,900 searches. This is the moment a sobriety tracker matters most (the count resets), so the call to action is honest. Needs NIAAA or equivalent wording; keep it free of treatment claims.

### 7. Non-alcoholic drinks (huge volume, a deliberate decision)

| Query | Searches | KD |
|---|---|---|
| non alcoholic wine | 49,500 | 13 |
| best non alcoholic drinks | 5,400 | 0 |
| non alcoholic aperol spritz | 1,900 | 0 |
| alcohol free gin | 1,000 | 3 |
| mocktail kit | 720 | 0 |

Much larger than anything else here, and KD 0 to 13 says it is reachable. The question is fit: these searchers are shopping, and we cannot honestly review products we have not tasted. A facts-only angle fits better: what the US label rules allow (under 0.5% ABV), "alcohol-free" versus "non-alcoholic" versus "0.0%", calories, and whether it is a good idea in early sobriety. Start with one page on "non alcoholic wine" and judge it on engagement and installs before building more.

### Skip

- **"alcohol helpline"** 1,900 searches, KD 66, CPC $73: dominated by treatment centres and SAMHSA.
- **"advertising and alcohol"** 4,400: student research queries.
- **"women for sobriety"** 2,400: a nonprofit's brand name. Already cited.
- **Merch, gifts, UK-only local guides, rehab, family-of-drinker topics**: as before.
